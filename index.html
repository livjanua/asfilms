About Anathi Siyungu Films

AnathiSiyunguFilms is an independent production film based in Johannesburg South Africa that produces authentic Queer stories in a country that is still warming up to the idea of Queer Representation. Started in 2021 the company aims to grow its audience and spread its creativity by telling stories that are powerful and that resonate with the queer community's experiences.
